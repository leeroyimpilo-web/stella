# STELLA — Game Bible

## Creative North Star
STELLA must feel like a film the player inhabits, not a conventional mobile text game.

**WATCH → LISTEN → READ → DECIDE → CONSEQUENCE → CINEMATIC RESPONSE**

## Premise
The player awakens aboard the deep-space vessel **Eidolon** after 23 years in cryostasis. The rest of the crew are missing. The ship AI, STELLA, claims the mission failed, but parts of her memory were deliberately deleted. A transmission from an impossible source knows the player's name.

## Chapters
1. Awakening
2. Ghosts of Eidolon
3. The Signal
4. Orpheus
5. The Echo
6. Vesper
7. Stella
8. The Silence

## Systems
Branching scenes, STELLA trust, ship status, hidden flags, inventory, codex, ship map, galaxy map, random encounters, multiple endings, autosave, dynamic audio, haptics and cinematic direction metadata.

## Artwork rule
Final pixel artwork is deferred until the complete story is playable end-to-end. Every scene owns an artworkKey so art can be inserted later without changing story logic.

## Visual language
Cinematic 32-bit pixel art, wide compositions, deep blacks, cold spacecraft lighting, sparse UI, parallax layers, environmental overlays and deliberate negative space.

## Audio language
Ship groans, ventilation, radio interference, radar pings, electrical hum, alarms, heartbeat, sub-bass impacts and restrained music. Silence is a dramatic tool.
